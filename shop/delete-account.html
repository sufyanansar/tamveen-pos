Delete your تَمْوِين account

This page is for the تَمْوِين grocery app by Tamveen Mini Market, Riyadh.

In the app

Open the app, go to Account, tap Delete account and confirm. It takes effect straight away.

You are signed in on this device as .

Without the app

Call the shop on +966 11 000 0000 from the phone number on your account, or tell us the email you signed in with, and we will delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, phone number and all saved addresses.
  • Your sign-in link with Apple or Google.
  • Any order that has not started yet is cancelled.

What is kept

Past order records (items, totals, date) are kept for as long as Saudi tax law requires, with your name replaced by "Deleted customer" and the address reduced to the district.
