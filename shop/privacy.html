Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

تَمْوِين (Tamveen) is the ordering app of Tamveen Mini Market, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia ("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, and your rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email from Sign in with Apple or Google, and an account identifier from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Contact: a phone number, if you add one, so the driver can call you.
  • Delivery addresses: the label, house, street, district, notes and the map point you set.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my location" while setting an address, and only while the app is open. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Orders: the items, totals, payment method (cash or card on delivery), notes and status history.

We do not collect payment card details in the app, do not show ads, and do not use tracking or advertising identifiers.

Why we use it

Only to take, prepare and deliver your orders, to show you your order history and live delivery tracking, and to contact you about an order. We keep invoices as required by Saudi tax (ZATCA) rules.

Who sees it

Our shop staff and our delivery drivers see the order, your name, phone and address. While your order is on the way, you see the driver's name and live position. Our data is stored with our hosting provider (Vercel) on our behalf. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your account and addresses are kept until you delete your account. Order records are kept for as long as tax law requires, with your name and address removed after you delete your account.

Your rights

You can see and correct your name, phone and addresses in the app under Account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account, Delete account) or on our account deletion page. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or raise any privacy question by calling +966 11 000 0000.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

سياسة الخصوصية

يمكنك تعديل بياناتك من صفحة الحساب، وحذف حسابك في أي وقت من التطبيق أو من صفحة حذف الحساب. للاستفسار اتصل على ‎+966 11 000 0000.
