Orders

An order is a request to buy. It is accepted when the shop starts preparing it. If an item is unavailable, the shop may remove it or call you. You can cancel an order in the app until the shop starts preparing it; after that, please call the shop.

Prices and payment

Prices are in Saudi riyals and include 15% VAT where it applies. Weighed items (fruit, vegetables, meat) are priced by the actual weight at billing, so the final total can differ slightly from the estimate. You pay the driver on delivery, in cash or by card. The minimum order, delivery fee and free-delivery threshold are shown in the basket.

Delivery

We deliver to addresses in our delivery area. Please make sure your address, map point and phone are correct and that someone is available to receive the order. If we cannot reach you, the driver may return the order to the shop.

Returns

Check your items at the door. If something is missing, damaged or wrong, tell the driver or call the shop the same day and we will fix it.

Your account
